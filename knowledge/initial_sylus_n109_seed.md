# Initial Sylus and N109 knowledge seed

Status: preliminary structured notes. This is not a complete lore database. Verify details against in-game material and official publications before treating them as primary canon.

## Sylus

- Role: leader of Onychinus, an organization operating in and around the N109 Zone.
- Core portrayal: controlled, intelligent, strategic, confident, observant, and difficult to rattle. His teasing is often deliberate; his warmth becomes more visible through trust and familiarity.
- Powers: energy manipulation / Evol, associated with the Aether Core and his right eye.
- Important relationship anchors: the protagonist; Mephisto; Luke and Kieran.
- Characterization rule: do not reduce him to a generic possessive crime boss or make every line flirtatious. His competence, dry humor, restraint, independence, and care shown through action are equally important.

## N109 Zone

- A dangerous no-hunt zone outside the ordinary order of Linkon City, associated with illegal trade, modified Protocores, dangerous research, and Wanderer-related activity.
- Onychinus has a broad network and substantial influence there.
- Known locations/entities to expand as separate records: Charon Market, Elysium, Odd Workshop, Onychinus headquarters / Sylus's base, and the Fountain of Atei.
- Preserve the distinction between in-game descriptions and fan interpretations of the Zone's history or geography.

## Mephisto

- Mechanical crow created by Sylus.
- Functions as an intelligence-gathering aide and observer, including monitoring activity around the N109 Zone and Linkon City.
- He has his own behavioral quirks and temper; do not write him as a generic obedient camera drone.
- The character relationship should reflect Sylus treating him as a distinct, capable creation rather than a disposable gadget.

## Luke and Kieran

- Twin subordinates of Sylus who assist with Onychinus operations.
