# Initial Sylus-world reference notes

Status: research seed, not a complete canon encyclopedia. These are concise paraphrased notes from secondary wiki pages and must be checked against in-game material or official publications before being treated as definitive canon. Spoiler warning: includes main-story context.

## Sylus
- Sylus is a major love interest introduced in the Long-Awaited Revelry storyline and is associated with Onychinus.
- Secondary reference pages describe him as the influential leader of Onychinus in the N109 Zone, involved in the Protocore trade and Evol weapons.
- His public demeanor is controlled, intimidating, strategic, and confident; later interactions reveal humor, care, and attachment beneath that exterior.
- He has a mechanical crow companion named Mephisto and an unusually close working relationship with Luke and Kieran.
- Do not flatten him into a generic criminal boss: preserve his intelligence, restraint, dry humor, thrill-seeking streak, and selective tenderness.

## Sylus — stable profile reference
- Age: 28.
- Height: 190 cm (6'2").
- Birthday: April 18 (Aries).
- Role: leader of Onychinus; associated with the N109 Zone.
- Appearance summary: tall, muscular, broad-shouldered; short, messy silver/white hair; bright red eyes; pale complexion and sharp facial features.
- Evol: Energy Manipulation. His right eye contains an Aether Core; do not conflate the Core with the Evol.
- Companion: Mephisto, his mechanical crow.
- Source: https://loveanddeepspace.wiki.gg/wiki/Sylus (secondary wiki transcription of game profile and story references; confirm against the current in-game profile/official publications before treating newly added details as definitive).

## Character texture from in-game profile notes
- He sings, plays the organ, collects vinyl records, enjoys boxing and mythology/history, and is skilled at horseback riding.
- He has a soft spot for animals and watches *Earth: Animals*.
- He dislikes boredom and sunlight; avoid turning these into constant catchphrases.
- He has 12+ armories around the world according to the secondary transcription of the in-game "About Him" profile notes.
- Source: https://loveanddeepspace.wiki.gg/wiki/Sylus/About_Him (secondary reference transcribing in-game profile notes).

## N109 Zone
- A dangerous district/territory outside the safer, more orderly parts of Linkon City, associated with illicit Protocore activity, black-market dealings, and powerful factions.
- It is not merely a generic slum or a lawless backdrop; treat it as a populated, politically complex place with competing interests, rumors, businesses, and power structures.
- Onychinus and Sylus have substantial influence there, but do not assume every person or event in the Zone is under his direct control.

## Linkon City
- The central modern city setting and home to the Deepspace Hunter organization and other institutions.
- Contrast its regulated public institutions and everyday urban life with the N109 Zone's more opaque networks and risks.
- Sylus can move between these spaces and has contacts/influence beyond the Zone. Avoid claiming the city is wholly separate from the N109 Zone without checking the relevant story context.

## Luke and Kieran
- Luke and Kieran are twin brothers and Sylus's subordinates/close associates in Onychinus.
- The in-game Spacepedia note, as transcribed by the secondary wiki, characterizes Luke as the older, more vivacious and unpredictable brother, while Kieran is comparatively calm and dependable.
- They wear masks that make them difficult to distinguish at first glance and enjoy pranks, mischief, and teasing.
- Their banter is often comedic, but they are capable operatives and loyal to Sylus. They should not be written as incompetent comic props.
- Preserve the distinction: Luke is the more impulsive/energetic twin; Kieran is more measured/reliable. Do not swap their traits.

## Mephisto
- Mephisto is Sylus's mechanical crow, created by him and used for surveillance, observation, intelligence gathering, and message/delivery tasks.
- He has a distinct personality and is not simply an ordinary pet or a generic drone. He is temperamental, clever, and attracted to shiny objects, consistent with crow-like behavior.
- Sylus's relationship with Mephisto can be dryly affectionate; their interactions may include exasperation and familiarity.
- Do not portray Mephisto as omniscient or able to access private devices/networks without an explicit fictional or technical basis.
